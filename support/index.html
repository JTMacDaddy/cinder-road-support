Game Support

Rowan’s Quest I: Cinder Road

For help, bug reports, or questions about the game, open a support request on GitHub. You’ll need a GitHub account to post.

Privacy: GitHub Issues and replies are public. Please do not include passwords, payment details, personal contact information, or other private information. For privacy questions, see our Privacy Policy.

What to include

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The game version shown in the app
  • What you were doing when the issue happened
  • Steps to reproduce it, if you know them
