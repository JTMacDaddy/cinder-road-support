Privacy Policy

Rowan’s Quest I: Cinder Road

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how JTMacDaddy (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Rowan’s Quest I: Cinder Road (“the game”).

Information handled by the game

The game stores gameplay progress and settings locally on your device. The game does not transmit this saved information to us, OpenAI/ChatGPT, or Runway. The current game version does not integrate advertising or analytics services. AI tools credited in the game’s production notices were used to help make game content; playing the game does not send your gameplay or personal information to those tools.

Local save data and deletion

Your local save remains on your device until you remove the app or its local data. Starting a new journey replaces active journey progress. The game may keep local recovery copies to protect save data; those copies are removed when the app’s local data is removed. Device or operating-system backups may retain separate copies according to your settings and the platform provider’s policies.

Platform services and external links

Apple and your operating system may process information under their own policies and settings, including information associated with app downloads, purchases, backups, or diagnostics. If you choose an external website link, that website’s privacy policy applies to your visit.

Contact and public support requests

For privacy questions or support, use our Support page or GitHub Issues page. GitHub Issues are public. Do not post passwords, payment details, or other private information there.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game or its data practices change. We will post updates at this URL and revise the effective date.
